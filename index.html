About

I'm an audio producer and sound designer with a growing focus on building the tools I wish existed — from custom audio software to interactive Pure Data patches. I care about how sound is made as much as how it's used, and I like moving between the creative and technical sides of a project.

This site collects the work I'm proudest of: produced tracks, sound design for media, software I've built for audio workflows, and patches that explore synthesis and signal processing in Pure Data. Explore each section below, and check back — I add new work regularly.
